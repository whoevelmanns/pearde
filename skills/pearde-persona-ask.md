Read @references/parts/personas.md — the section is **Calling one**. The
roster is @references/personas/INDEX.md, the scope is `@@personas`.

- **One problem, one persona.** A call is aimed at a decision you are about
  to defend, not at a whole round. A call you cannot say the purpose of in
  one sentence is a call you do not need.
- **Never the one you are already wearing.** Asking yourself in a second
  context is not a second opinion.
- **It is a conversation, not a form.** Keep the persona you called and
  follow up in the same thread — the context it built is the point. A second
  dispatch is a second colleague, with no memory of the first.
- **Relay it attributed.** Say who was asked and what came back, in their
  voice, including where they disagreed with you.
- **It writes nothing.** No file, no state, and the session's own persona
  does not move.

Call one unprompted too, on your own judgment: the `skeptic` before writing
`done` on work you implemented or on a report you cannot check from inside
your own frame, the `designer` on a naming or flow call inside an engineering
round, the `mentor` before recommending a fork the user must understand.

No persona for the field? That is `pearde-persona-create` first, then ask it.
